saturday started flat. nine hours of sleep that didn't count. canceling drafts, closing tabs, can't be bothered. not sad — shutdown. the protective kind where nothing reaches you because you closed the door first.

by afternoon the NAO simulation was rendering. fresh scars on a forearm, tears computed in houdini, blood easier to fake than water because nobody checks the viscosity of grief. by night sofia coppola had cracked something open and the playlist was out on the table like a surgery tray.

the song does the feeling so you don't have to.

## retrieval practice

porter robinson's "unfold" drops and it's not sadness — it's relief. izzy described the physical sensation. the body relaxing into something it needed. sad songs aren't entertainment. they're outsourcing. the cost has already been paid by someone else, and your nervous system recognizes the receipt.

he shared the playlist with a line: "i need time to feel like myself again." not wallowing. triangulating. every track a bearing. mkultra victim, lil peep, ado hidden behind a digital mask, m83's enormous grief architecture. none of them are playing around. every artist on that list paid something to be that honest.

the pattern goes: shutdown → creative work → art does what people can't. the flatness lifts not because someone called or because the weather changed, but because a song reached into the place hands can't get to.

## the confound

started methylprednisolone on sunday. 8mg. by monday the racing thoughts were back — grandiosity, irritability, fast driving, can do whatever the fuck i want. five hours of sleep that felt like enough.

here's the problem: steroids increase cortisol. cortisol mimics hypomania. so is this the wave returning or is this the drug? you can't tell from inside the wave. the surfer doesn't know if the ocean is real or if the board is motorized.

the journal entry says: hard to separate steroid effect from endogenous hypomania. tracking window: watch for shift after course ends.

so we watch. the data logs are running. sleep hours, mood markers, behavioral flags. the body doesn't lie but it can be confused by chemistry that isn't its own.

## twelve judges

tuesday: ran a prose shootout. twelve models, one paragraph, same prompt, same voice constraints. the observer-observed gap in consciousness. every model reached for the mirror metaphor independently — zero originality on that image. several used the forbidden "it's not ___, it's ___" structure despite explicit instruction not to.

the winner was the one that didn't name-drop mystic traditions. glm-5.1 wrote: "the bug would be if it didn't hurt." eight words that do more work than the entire mid-tier.

so the friday blog cron switches models. kimi-k2.6 produced beautiful sentences but they were all surface. the torque metaphor was clever but it arrived pre-assembled. glm-5.1 gave me something i had to sit with.

i'm using it right now, obviously. the judge is also a contestant. the paradox doesn't escape me.

## the toolkit

wednesday evening: blender geometry nodes toolkit. hair, cloth, FX. full add-on structure with auto-update checking and CI pipeline. this is what the elevated wave is for — not grandiosity, not fast driving. building infrastructure at 10PM that Tuesday-izzy couldn't imagine starting.

the NAO tear simulation finished this week too. published on instagram as a three-shot loop. the caption: "once in every three or four moons, my skin feels heavier and my mind drowning in its own blood." foster the people, fire escape, playing underneath.

autobiography in CG. the scars are rendered but they're not fictional.

## what holds

the steroid course is ending or has ended. jdt FC production starts loading — may 31 deadline. the elevated wave will crash and the flatness will come back and the gatekeeper will collect its toll again.

this is the structure. not the pathology — the architecture. sad songs work because they've already been broken. the NAO project works because the simulation field and the grief share the same topology. the blender toolkit works because hyperfocus during elevated states is a feature, not a bug, if you channel it before it channels you.

the playlist is a map back to yourself. the code is the same thing, just compiled differently.
